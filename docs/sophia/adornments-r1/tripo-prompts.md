# Tripo3D — isolated ornament generation (ONLY if Work reports UN-SALVAGEABLE)

Official Tripo Studio supports multi-view input and Smart Mesh, segmentation, UV, texturing and rigging. For this workflow **do not auto-rig the whole Sophia**. Generate isolated meshes and export mesh/UV/PBR for transfer to the existing authorial rig by Work/Blender.

## Before spending credits
- Work source lock must identify a specific missing/irreparable accessory. If only Book/Pen or forehead star is needed, use original mesh or procedural low-poly instead.
- Prepare **2–4 orthographic images of the SAME object**, matching front/right/back/left shape, color, scale and decorations. No poses, characters or scene in the reference. Work should produce the image sheet from a verified reference, not invent it.
- Save prompt, model/version/credits, image SHA hashes, exported GLB/FBX, maps and screenshots. No direct deployment from Tripo output.

## Prompt A — detachable laboratory coat (conditional)
"Create ONLY one separate anthropomorphic-female laboratory COAT GARMENT, not a human or animal body. Clean hollow white medical/research coat shell intended to fit an existing slender upright anthropomorphic fox character in neutral A-pose. Modestly open front, shaped lapels, correctly connected shoulder seams, long sleeves with defined cuffs, interior opening and visible inner lining, slightly flared lower hem, delicate gold piping matching the supplied design reference. Natural garment construction and clean game-ready topology with UV and PBR. Preserve front, profile and back consistency. Neutral A-pose with both sleeves extended, without arm or hand geometry. No face, hair, fox ears, tail, legs, human body, mannequin, gloves, book, pen, straps, rocket, stand, halo or background scenery. One isolated coat, separate mesh pieces where useful for lapels/sleeves, no autogenerated skeleton. Prioritize silhouette and animation-friendly joint flow over raw polygon count."

**Important:** a free-standing garment may still be filled/closed incorrectly by image-to-3D. Do not accept geometry with filled neck, arms, torso or closed sleeves. Compare topology and fit before any rigging/export.

## Prompt B — ear ornament (only if no salvage)
"One isolated symmetrical PAIR of small decorative ear-base ornaments for a stylized anthropomorphic fox character. Use only the supplied orthographic concept references. Delicate pale-gold metal rings and minimal white/blue insets; slim and low-poly with realistic thickness and separable left/right objects. No fox ears or fur, no head, character, extra jewelry, ribbons, hands, body, pedestal or scene. Preserve shape and decorative design exactly from references. Provide UV and optimized game-ready geometry, no rig or animations."

## Prompt C — headphones (music variant only if needed)
"Isolated pair of fitted headphones, designed around tall upright fox ears without clipping. Light cream/white and restrained blue-gold accents matching the supplied reference, separate headband and ear cups with accurate thickness and topology; no actual fox head/ears, hair, face, humanoid, clothing, body or scenic background. Game-ready mesh, UV/PBR, no skeleton, no physics."

## Rejection criteria before export
- Character/body regenerated; source silhouette altered; ears fused with jewelry; textures painted as geometry; thick disconnected coat sleeves; no opening for the torso; asymmetry between consistent orthos; huge triangles; invalid UVs or impossible cloth fit.
- Post-export: Work measures pivots and bounds, cleans normals, repairs loops, applies retopology if necessary and skins the coat against the existing body. Export format is not evidence of compatible weights.
- No assumption that Tripo credit cost, free-plan privacy or rig capabilities are unchanged: inspect your account before generating.

# Prompt da arte da sala de escuta

Modo: geração de imagem integrada, com cinco referências (Phrolova, Hsin, duas de Shorekeeper e primeiro quadro da Sophia ativa no Work). Primeira saída: 1672 × 941 px. Uma segunda geração estendeu o céu para liberar os controles; saída final: 1536 × 1024 px (3:2), mantendo as personagens e a cena na parte inferior.

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Create a NEW high-resolution wide landscape anime illustration, 16:9 composition, ideally 3840x2160, for a music section on a personal website. Supplied images are identity and drawing references, NOT a collage to reproduce. Reference 1: Phrolova, long silver-grey hair, fringe partly over one eye, crimson eyes, crimson/black/ivory structured clothing and ribboned conductor baton. Reference 2: Hsin, silver-white hair with charcoal tips, tall upright narrow fox ears with dark pointed tips, red-gold eyes, small crimson forehead mark, black/crimson/gold Chinese-inspired clothing and jewelry. References 3 and 4: Shorekeeper, pale blue hair, VIOLET eyes (NOT blue), white and deep blue iridescent dress, white/deep-blue hood/veil, cyan butterfly motifs. Reference 5: ORIGINAL Sophia mascot, use its ivory fur/hair, deep midnight-blue ear tips and fluffy star-speckled midnight-blue tail, warm amber-gold eyes and gold forehead star as the exact palette/identity translated into a HUMAN ANIME FOX GIRL. Sophia remains distinct from Hsin but resembles Hsin in tall narrow fox ears, youthful about 17 years old, fully and modestly dressed in ivory and midnight-blue clothing with subtle gold details.
Composition planned for live HTML widgets: reserve BOTH upper corners as quiet uncluttered sky, leftmost 28% and rightmost 28% of the upper 28% of the image, no faces/hands/critical details there. JUST natural sky, do not draw UI, text, paper, frames or widgets. Central moon extends between these zones. Left: Phrolova about x=16%, head y=40%, three-quarter view, crimson conductor baton, looking right; crimson accents. Right: Shorekeeper x=84%, head y=42%, three-quarter view, looking left; cool cyan/deep-blue accents. Opposing forces in a single landscape, not separate portrait panels. Center: Hsin x=50%, head y=35%, full moon behind her, face and fox ears clearly featured, central protagonist in a human-scale three-quarter figure, NO giant floating disembodied head. Her fox form prominently visible near her center-left below: a majestic distinct silver-white fox with charcoal ear tips, amber-red eyes, subtle crimson/gold ornamentation and a sweeping pale fluffy tail with dark tip; readable anatomy. Foreground slightly right of center x=61%, y=78%: Sophia sits on a stone terrace, comfortably scaled and visible head to feet, premium midnight-blue/gold headphones, open book in lap; she pauses reading and looks up across the moonlit landscape. Long ivory hair, navy fox-ear tips, amber eyes and midnight-blue star-speckled tail identify her. Beside Sophia a SMALL WHITE FOX with subtle crimson ear markings and crimson tail-tip details. Distinct from Hsin's larger fox. Sophia and foxes do not obscure canonical faces.
Art direction: finely drawn contemporary Japanese anime key visual, thin clean contour lines, crisp source-accurate faces/eyes, controlled cel shading with delicate gradients. Correct hands FIVE fingers, BLUE nails on Shorekeeper's visible hand, crimson nails on Phrolova. Not pixel art, not oily/photorealistic 3D, not generic AI fantasy. Source design fidelity especially Phrolova's fringe, Hsin's ears and Shorekeeper's hood, violet eyes and nails. A single moonlit stone terrace, calm reflective lake and soft distant traditional architecture connects the scene. Color transitions crimson left -> moonlit ivory/gold center -> cyan/midnight-blue right. Limited petals/butterflies. Subordinate architecture, spacious sky, no chaotic glitter, no lace-like texture noise, no excessive sparkle/overexposure. Crisp readable at 1200px wide. Anatomically clean and contemplative. NO text, logos, watermarks, borders, UI or paper. Exactly FOUR human characters: Phrolova left, Hsin center, Shorekeeper right, Sophia foreground. Exactly TWO foxes: Hsin's large fox center-left and Sophia's small white/red companion.
```


Refinamento: extensão somente do céu superior, sem UI gravada na arte, mantendo a identidade das quatro personagens e das duas raposas. O céu dos cantos foi reservado para os controles reais.
